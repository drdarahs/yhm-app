YHM Coach Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026. YHM Coach is the training app of the Yavatmal Health Marathon, organised by Cotton City Runners Foundation, Yavatmal, Maharashtra.

What the app stores

Your profile (name, age, gender, weight, height, goal, health conditions and habits you choose to enter), your training plan, checklists and activity log. All of this is stored only on your phone, inside the app. It is not uploaded to any server and is not shared with the organisers or anyone else.

Health Connect data

If you choose to connect Health Connect, the app reads these data types:

  • Exercise sessions: to add your runs, walks and workouts (type, date, duration, distance) to your activity log.
  • Distance and heart rate: to show the distance and average heart rate of each workout.
  • Steps: to show today's step count.
  • Weight: to help keep your nutrition targets up to date.

The app only reads this data. It never writes to Health Connect, never sends this data off your phone, and never uses it for advertising. Data read from Health Connect is used only to show your training progress and personalise your plan inside the app. You can turn off access at any time in Health Connect settings.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No information is sent to a server to deliver them.

Race updates

The app downloads the public race updates list from yavatmalhealthmarathon.com. This download does not include any of your data.

Deleting your data

Use Clear my data in your profile, or uninstall the app. Both remove everything the app stored.

Medical note

YHM Coach gives general training and nutrition guidance. It is not a medical service. Follow your doctor's advice first.
