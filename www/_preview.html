How to export an activity
  • Garmin Connect: open the activity on connect.garmin.com → gear icon → Export to GPX or TCX. For many runs: Activities → Export CSV.
  • Strava: open the activity on strava.com → ⋯ menu → Export GPX.
  • Google Fit: takeout.google.com → Fit → download, then upload the TCX files from the Activities folder.
  • Samsung Health: Settings → Download personal data, then upload the exercise CSV.
